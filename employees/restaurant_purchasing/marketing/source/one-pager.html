How it works

  1. Count. Staff enter what is on the shelf, or say it in chat.
  2. Order. Purchase orders to par, cheapest supplier per item, every supplier's minimum met.
  3. Approve. Orders under your limit go out by email. Bigger ones wait for the owner.
  4. Check. Each invoice is matched to its order; overcharges become a credit request.

You stay in control

  • Dollar limit for automatic orders
  • Manager sign-off on credit requests
  • Every order, approval and email logged
  • Your own workspace, isolated at the database

What it catches

  • A cheaper supplier for the same item, compared per unit
  • Orders that would miss a supplier's minimum
  • Prices billed above the agreed price
  • Extra cases and items you never ordered
